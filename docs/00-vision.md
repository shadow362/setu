# Vision

## Why Setu Exists

Setu was born from a simple personal problem.

I often discover useful information on my phone while I'm away from my computer, or I find something on my computer that I'll need later on my phone. By the time I'm back at the other device, I've either forgotten about it, lost the link, or have to search for it again.

Existing solutions often require messaging myself, relying on browser history, or keeping both devices connected at the same time. I wanted something simpler.

Setu is being built to solve that problem.

---

## Vision Statement

Setu is an open-source personal cross-device inbox that helps people intentionally send information between their own devices, allowing them to continue where they left off whenever they return.

---

## The Problem

People frequently discover useful information on one device while working on another.

Whether it's a link, a note, or a small piece of text, there isn't always a simple and reliable way to make that information available on another device later. Existing workflows are often fragmented, require unnecessary steps, or depend on remembering to retrieve the information later.

---

## Who Is Setu For?

The first user of Setu is its creator.

It is designed for people who regularly switch between a phone and a computer and want a simple way to move information between their own devices without disrupting their workflow.

---

## Core Idea

Capture information on one device.

Continue your work on another.

Nothing more. Nothing less.

---

## Guiding Philosophy

Setu is intentionally simple.

Every feature should help users move information between their own devices quickly and reliably. If a feature does not improve that core experience, it should be carefully questioned before being added.

The goal is not to become another messaging app or note-taking platform, but to become the easiest way to continue your work across devices.
